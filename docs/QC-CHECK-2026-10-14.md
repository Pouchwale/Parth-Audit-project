# QC formats held against their papers, 14-Oct-2026 (REQUIREMENTS §111)

The owner, 14-Oct-2026: "check all documents of QC only for now and make sure each and everything written in document
should be there present there properly." Every QC format DCRS holds (F-QC-01 to F-QC-40.B; F-QC-31 is SAP-generated and
not wanted, F-QC-39 is kept in SAP, F-QC-41 is obsolete) was held against its original once more, on the code of
integrate-14oct (0ed2b0e), after §111 parts 1 to 8.

**How.** Each paper's text was read out of its PDF with its positions on the page (unpdf, item by item, line by line),
the Excel workbook of F-QC-14 cell by cell, the two Word statements paragraph by paragraph, and the photographed papers
(F/QC/13, the Gujarati line clearance, F-QC-30, F-QC-32, F-QC-40.C) looked at. On the DCRS side every QC document's
definition and layout was written out through the same code the pages read (`documentRepository`, `getLogSheetLayout`):
name and number, revision, the boxes above and below the grid in their order with their printed labels, printed values,
choices across, tables and groups, the columns in their order with their headings, groups, units and bands, the fixed
lines word for word, the lines printed above and below the grid and at the foot, and the reference tables. The two were
compared box by box, column by column, line by line.

**What was checked on every document.** Title; format number and revision; every header box, its label and its place;
every column and its order; every fixed line and its words; units, specifications and tolerances; the notes above and
below the grid; the footer lines; the signature boxes and the words printed under them; that every box the paper leaves
to be written is a box here (editable), and that a value the paper prints is printed, not typed.
