# DCRS audit — 7 October 2026

**What was checked:** the `premium-theme` branch at commit `2833a2a`. It is the newest integrated code; `main` (`576d873`) is 12 commits behind it.

**How:** nine read-only reviews, each covering one area:
- the server and its security;
- the /api/v1 interface and operations;
- the browser's data layer;
- the rules engine;
- analytics;
- Mitra and the voice;
- the screens and the theme;
- the paper forms and the Production module;
- the repository, the tests and the documents.

Findings from an earlier pass on 3 October were checked again against today's code. Every critical and high finding below was confirmed by reading the code a second time.

**Not done:** no code was changed, and the browser suites were not run.

## Verdict

DCRS is a large and careful piece of work. The typecheck passes and almost every unit test passes. The design is thoughtful:
- one lateness rule shared by the browser and the server;
- saves checked against the stored version, with three-way merges;
- department scoping enforced on the server;
- parameterised SQL;
- safe CSV and Excel export;
- zip-bomb limits on uploads.

It is not yet safe to run a plant on. What stands in the way:

- **A capacity wall.** Every browser keeps every company record in one storage item, which a year of real use will overflow (C-1).
- **Work not yet on GitHub.** Seven finished commits exist on this PC alone, and one branch's work is not even committed (C-2).
- **Records that say what nobody did.** Clearances and ALC answers are pre-filled, signatures are filled or invented, stock counts are copied from yesterday, and the assistant's drafts are counted in reports (H-9 to H-13).
- **Mitra signing without asking.** The agent can submit and verify with no review (H-1).
- **Changes that can be silently lost or overwritten.** See H-3 to H-5.
- **Server holes** usable from the plant's network (H-14 to H-17).
- **No backup.** The setup guide says one runs every evening, but none is scheduled (H-19).

## Issues

Every item has an id that the fix prompt at the end uses. The original reviewers' ids are in square brackets.

### Critical

**C-1. Every browser keeps the whole company's records in one storage item that a year of use will overflow** [data-1, apidb-3]

- `frontend/src/data/storageAdapter.ts:181-183` sets a 4,000,000-character warning mark and a 5,000,000-character wall.
- At `frontend/src/data/serverSync.ts:1035`, a sign-in that cannot fit throws `no-room`.
- `frontend/src/main.tsx:35` tells the person to "archive older records", but no records archive exists. Only the activity log can be archived.
- A filled month is about 480,000 characters. The six Production daily sheets of §91 add about 160,000 more (`frontend/src/data/demoGenerator.ts:510-518`).
- **Projection:** an account that sees every department (super admin, management, QA, MR) reaches the warning after about 6 months of live use and the wall after about 7 to 8. From then on, that browser cannot sign in and saves fail. The figures come from the code's own measurements of demo data, not from a real year.
- **Related [data-9]:** every save makes every open browser, the saver's own included, download and parse the whole item again. `serverSync.ts:657` ignores the sequence number that the save returns.
- **What the fix must achieve:** keep the browser's copy bounded for good. One way is a window of months in the browser, with older periods fetched from PostgreSQL. Another is IndexedDB with records synced one by one. Either way, add a real records archive behind the on-screen advice, and a test that signs in a whole-company account with a working copy of the projected size.

**C-2. Finished work exists only on this PC** [repo-1]

- `performance-score` has 6 commits (§92, the minus score) with no upstream; no remote branch exists.
- `superadmin-any-time` has 1 commit (`915735b`) with no upstream.
- The `mitra-fill` worktree (`C:\Users\parth\Desktop\dcrs-fill-wt`) holds 24 uncommitted changes, among them `fillPlan.ts`, `fillRequest.ts` and their tests.
- A disk failure, a forced worktree removal or a clean-up of the Desktop folders loses that work. It also breaks the owner's rule that every change is committed and pushed.
- The parallel session may still be working on these branches. Push the committed ones (pushing is not destructive), and ask that session to commit and push its worktree. Never remove a worktree whose `git status` shows changes.

### High

**H-1. Mitra's agent submits, verifies, approves, sends back and cancels corrections with no review and no confirmation** [mitra-1, engine-2]

- `frontend/src/engine/mitraTools.ts:696-730`: `record_action` calls the page's submit, verify, approve, send-back and cancel-correction directly. Only delete asks first (`ctx.confirm` at :713).
- The agent's own prompt says send-back confirms (`backend/mitraAgent.ts:122`), but it does not.
- `parallel_tool_calls` is on (`backend/groq.ts:277`), so "fill it with sample data and submit it" can do both in one round.
- Text the model reads, such as a remark on a record or a line in a PDF, can also trigger these calls. Only attachment text is declared to be data (`backend/mitraAgent.ts:127-130`).
- This breaks §35 (a review step, with "I've checked it — submit" as the only way through) and §80.
- **The fix:** every signing step waits for the person's yes; submit uses §35's review step; record text in tool results is declared data, never instructions.

**H-2. In the docked chat, nobody can answer a tool's yes/no question, so reopen, delete and save-as-revision hang until Stop** [mitra-2]

- At `frontend/src/components/common/DocumentAssistant.tsx:1814-1831`, `send()` returns while the turn is busy, before it looks for a waiting question.
- The chips, a typed yes and a spoken yes are all dropped.
- **The fix:** the waiting question can be answered while its turn runs, with an e2e test that taps Yes on an agent confirmation in the dock.

**H-3. Life-cycle steps run on whatever copy the page holds and never check the record's current status** [engine-1]

- `frontend/src/engine/recordLifecycle.ts:128-204` never checks the status.
- `frontend/src/pages/GapPage.tsx:353-390` and `frontend/src/pages/TrainingPage.tsx:143-268` pass copies loaded when the page opened.
- **Example:** a stale Send back on a gap inspection that was verified in the meantime erases the verification and its history entry.
- `RecordPage` and the phone API already check (`RecordPage.tsx:398-447`, `engineHost/entry.ts:1446-1490`).
- **The fix:** every step runs on the stored record and is refused from the wrong status:
  - submit only from Scheduled, Due or In Progress;
  - verify and send back only from Submitted or Pending Verification;
  - resume only from Rejected.

**H-4. An open CAPA page saves its old list of findings over the current one, so a finding closed from the phone opens again** [apidb-1; REQUIREMENTS confirmation item 39]

- `frontend/src/pages/GapPage.tsx:315-320` builds every finding change from `record.data` as it was loaded.
- `frontend/src/App.tsx:126` keys the page by record id only.
- `docs/chatbot-integration.md:885` lists this under "Known limits".
- **The fix:** build every change from the stored record, then remove the known-limit paragraph.

**H-5. When two browsers change one record, the whole record with the later client-clock time wins** [data-2]

- See `frontend/src/data/serverSync.ts:555-560` and `recordRepository.ts:182`.
- A submission, its signature and the other person's history lines can vanish. §65 promises that the history shows what happened.
- **The fix:**
  - merge the history by entry id;
  - never move a status backwards in a merge;
  - add a history line saying a merge happened;
  - order by a server-assigned version, not the client's clock;
  - tell the person whose edit lost.

**H-6. The browser's own clock decides "today", and one PC with a wrong date moves the plant's go-live floor for good** [data-3]

- See `frontend/src/utils/date.ts:25-27`, `frontend/src/data/repositories/settingsRepository.ts:153-160` and `serverSync.ts:587-590`.
- The live-start date is an ordinary company key that any client can write (`backend/index.ts:1333`).
- A laptop showing 2025 creates a year of live "due" shells for everyone.
- **The fix:**
  - take "today" from the server, in the plant's time zone (`/auth/me` already sends the offset);
  - refuse to generate records when the clock is far off;
  - make live-start server-owned and set once.

**H-7. "New record" on a weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly document files a second sheet for a period that already has one** [engine-3]

- `frontend/src/engine/recordCrud.ts:72` matches on `periodKey = documentId:exactDate` (`recordGenerator.ts:90-92`). So a monthly sheet generated for the 25th is not found on the 7th.
- **The fix:** a schedule period (week, fortnight, month, quarter or year) counts as covered, and "New record" opens its record. Add a test.

**H-8. A history entry keeps the before and after values of only 60 changes; the rest are only counted** [engine-5]

- See `frontend/src/engine/recordHistory.ts:48` and `:256-259`.
- An Excel upload or a sample fill of a 144-cell sheet keeps the values of only 60 cells. Merged saves also lose the original "before" values.
- **The fix:** store every change with its before and after values, and limit only what is displayed.

**H-9. F/PRD/21's Line Clearance starts as "Done", and ALC starts as "Yes", before anyone answers** [formats-1]

- `frontend/src/data/seed/productionLayouts.ts:253` defaults the clearance to "Done", and `:108-115` defaults ALC to "Yes". `logSheetLayouts.ts:203` does the same for F-PRD-18.
- `frontend/src/engine/recordDefaults.ts:170` copies non-text defaults into every blank row.
- On the paper, and on F/QC/15, these boxes are the operator's to answer. Here the required check can never catch an unanswered clearance.
- **The fix:** these start empty, as decisions, the way Lot Status already does.

**H-10. The blade and sharp-object records copy yesterday's line** [formats-2]

- This affects the F/PRD/22 and F/PRD/24 blade stock, and the issued, returned, broken and new counts of F/PRD/10 and F/PRD/23. See `productionLayouts.ts:121-228`, `autoFill.ts:514-528` and `:860-868`.
- The paper is a running stock ledger:
  - opening stock does not follow from the previous closing;
  - a broken blade, which is a foreign-body event, appears again on days when nothing broke.
- **The fix:** counts and incidents are never copied. Opening stock comes from the previous closing, and incidents start empty.

**H-11. F-PRD-18's OPERATOR SIGN is filled in by the assistant ("Gaurav Singh")** [formats-3]

- `frontend/src/data/seed/logSheetLayouts.ts:204` sets `autoFill: { sign: true }`, against §91.4. The sister sheets F-PRD-20 and F-PRD-26 leave it blank.
- `frontend/tests/production.test.ts:484` skips columns marked `sign`, so the test passes anyway.
- **The fix:** the operator signs, and the test checks every signature column.

**H-12. F/DISP/04's Driver sign is filled with an invented driver and carried forward** [formats-4]

- See `frontend/src/data/seed/dispatchLayouts.ts:193` (`carryForward`) and `:199` ("Rameshbhai Patel").
- No filled copy was supplied, and that name is on no register.
- **The fix:** leave it blank, never copied from a specimen or from the last sheet, and remove the invented person.

**H-13. Reports and the Dashboard count Mitra's unconfirmed drafts, including simulated rodent catches and readings, as plant figures** [analytics-1]

- `frontend/src/data/selectors.ts:99-104` (`rodentsInMonth`) has no status filter, and neither does `:202` (`dailyRecordFilled`).
- `frontend/src/pages/ReportsPage.tsx:175` counts In Progress records.
- `frontend/src/engine/autoFill.ts:185` writes a simulated catch into the prepared draft.
- A printed rodent trend can show a catch nobody saw. The Management Summary already uses the human-record rule.
- **The fix:** every report, tile and trend sheet uses that rule (`insights.ts`, `isHumanRecord`).

**H-14. A crafted Word or Excel file freezes the whole server** [backend-1]

- `backend/zip.ts:150` runs `.replace(/<[^>]+>/g, "")` on up to 20 MB of inflated XML.
- The time grows with the square of the text: about 9 seconds at 10,000 characters and 48 seconds at 20,000.
- It is reachable through `/api/assistant/extract` (`backend/index.ts:1001`, not throttled) and through the CV reader.
- **The fix:** tags are stripped in linear time, with a size and a time limit, and file reading runs off the request thread or is capped per account.

**H-15. A storage write without a base version overwrites company data unconditionally, with any JSON and no activity line** [backend-2]

- `backend/index.ts:1477-1478` treats a missing `X-Base-Version` as "no check", and `backend/db.ts:906` skips the comparison.
- Any signed-in account can script-replace formats or master data, or forge record statuses in its own department, without a trace.
- **The fix:**
  - refuse company writes without a base version;
  - check the stored shape and the life-cycle transitions;
  - write an activity line for each company write.

**H-16. The account-management routes skip the first-password lock** [backend-3]

- `backend/index.ts:400-412` (`requireAdmin`) has no `mustChangePassword` check; `requireAuth` has one at :217.
- The built-in first password is documented.
- On a fresh install, anyone on the LAN can sign in as the super admin at any hour and create accounts or reset passwords before the owner changes it.
- **The fix:** the same lock on every admin route, with a test.

**H-17. Anyone on the network can add permanent rows to the activity log without an account** [backend-4]

- Refused sign-ups are logged before the throttle (`backend/index.ts:319`).
- Failed sign-ins are logged for each new made-up email (:583), because the throttle is keyed by email.
- The log is append-only, with five indexes and a trigram index.
- **The fix:** rate-limit unauthenticated refusals by IP address, and do not write a row for each one.

**H-18. Once the shared database is set up, the Audit Assistant's role could put tables in front of DCRS's own** [apidb-2]

- DCRS connects as the superuser and never pins `search_path` (`backend/db.ts:144` and `:451`).
- Part 1 grants `audit_assistant` CREATE on the database (`database/sql/01-schemas-and-roles.sql:96`).
- The danger is latent until part 1 is applied (item 37).
- **The fix, before that:** pin `search_path` on every DCRS connection, or schema-qualify DCRS's SQL. Add a database test with a look-alike schema.

**H-19. The plant setup guide says a backup runs every evening, but none is scheduled** [apidb-4]

- `docs/phone-app-setup.md:233` makes the claim, while `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md:5098` says the schedule is "NOT registered".
- With its defaults, the S4U task cannot reach the network share the documents recommend, and the failure is silent (`scripts/database/schedule-backup.ps1:60`, `backup.ts:156-161`).
- **The fix:**
  - the guide states what really runs;
  - a share can be reached;
  - the super admin sees a warning when the newest backup is older than a day.
- Registering the task on the real database is the owner's decision.

## Decisions only the owner can make

Each has a default in the code today, and nothing listed here should be built until the owner answers.

1. **Browser storage capacity (C-1).** Which design: a window of months in the browser, or IndexedDB with record-by-record sync?
