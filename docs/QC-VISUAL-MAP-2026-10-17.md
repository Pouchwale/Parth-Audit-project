## How a document reaches the paper

- **One component draws the screen, the preview, the print and the downloads.** Every QC format except the three Statements of Compliance is a `log-sheet` document: `RecordPage` (a record, route `#/record/<id>`) and `DocumentRecordsPage` (the document's own page, `#/document/<id>`) both mount `LogSheetRecordView`, which draws the sheet in three parts - the header block (`DocumentHeader`, in `.paper-head`), the body (`.paper-body`: the boxes, the grid, the notes) and the foot (`.paper-foot`). Printing goes through `frontend/src/utils/print.ts` (`printDocument()` and the `beforeprint` listener: scoping to `[data-print-doc]`, the fit to A4, the `@page` orientation, `data-paper`).
- **The layout is data.** `getLogSheetLayout(id)` (frontend/src/data/seed/logSheetLayouts.ts) merges the per-family files; the plant's own change to a format (`data/formatEdits.ts`) is laid over the issued one. Nothing is drawn per document in the component, except the grid kinds named in the table (`AcrossGrid`, `AnswerAcrossGrid`, `PanelGrid`, `HeadingBox`, `BoxTable`, `PrintedTable`, `NotesBelow`, `SheetPhotos`).
- **The Statements of Compliance** are reference-only (`isReferenceOnly`): `ComplianceDetailPage` draws the statement from `complianceStatements.ts`; its paper is a Word file the owner converted to PDF.

## What is compared

A BLANK NEW record printed on A4 by the app's own print path (`beforeprint`, print media, `prefer_css_page_size`), against the paper's pages. The paper is the truth for layout, words, order, widths, borders, fonts, row counts and orientation. The owner's explicit overrides stay (REQUIREMENTS section 111 and 117): no DATE cell in the header block, every fillable box editable, F/QC/12 standard weights, carry-forward from the previous record, one F/QC/12 record per week, and the system-generated footer lines. Never invent data in a record (section 98). The kit is `tests/qc_visual/`; see its README.md.
